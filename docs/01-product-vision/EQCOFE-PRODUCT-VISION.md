# EQCOFE Product Vision

## Identity
- Name: EQCOFE
- Meaning: Equipment Coffee
- Product: e-commerce for café/coffee equipment and accessories
- Language/UX: Persian-first, RTL

## Customers
- Coffee enthusiasts and home users
- Café owners/operators
- Approved B2B/wholesale customers

## Initial catalog vision
Approximately 200 initial products across brewing equipment, cold-bar tools, hot-bar tools, mugs/drinkware and related accessories.

## Explicit exclusions from the retail vision
- Coffee beans / ground coffee
- Industrial espresso machines

## Experience vision
Fast Persian storefront, strong search/filtering, product comparison (maximum four products in the same category), rich media including video/3D where available, account/order/after-sales flows, wholesale pricing after approval, and a powerful audited admin system.

## Launch philosophy
Deliver a secure, recoverable MVP first. Advanced marketplace integrations, personalization and non-critical AI/marketing capabilities must not unnecessarily block launch.
