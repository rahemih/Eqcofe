## Primary and recovery flow

The primary path is Home → Category or Search → Listing/Filter → Product Detail handoff. Product Detail itself remains owned by 55-C and is not drawn here. The recovery path never discards safe context without an explicit customer action: query, category, filters, sorting and previous listing position remain visible or restorable.

No-result distinguishes a valid but empty query from a provider/network failure. Offline preserves the search phrase. Timeout offers one bounded retry and a path back to the previous result. Failed state never displays fake success and may expose a copyable support reference. Disabled controls state their reason next to the control and do not pretend to implement server authorization.

## Responsive behavior

The inherited mobile-first widths are 320, 360, 600, 840, 1200 and 1440px. Compact layouts use the four-column grid, a two-row shell when search needs full width, one-column product cards and a full-height filter drawer. At 600px the eight-column grid permits two-column listings while keeping filters in a drawer. At 840px and wider, the twelve-column grid exposes navigation and search together; filters become a three-column aside and results use three or four columns according to capacity. Content remains capped at 1280px at 1440.

The responsive companions explicitly review every required width. The 320 and 1440 SVGs are anchor evidence rather than a claim that only those two widths matter. At 400% zoom the layout returns to the compact reading order, keeps one-dimensional page scroll and does not hide the primary task or authoritative state.

## Persian, RTL and accessibility evidence

The frames use realistic Persian labels, grouped integer Toman examples and bidi-safe identifier policy. No lorem ipsum, Wallet terminology, Brown palette, dark theme, invented logo, provider claim or fabricated product media is present. Media and brand positions are neutral labelled placeholders only.

The source order follows meaning in RTL: skip link, approved-wordmark slot/home, search, account, cart, navigation, H1, query/filter controls, results, pagination and footer. Interactive targets are at least 44×44px. Visible focus, one H1, logical landmarks, non-color state text, persistent form labels, status announcements and bounded recovery are acceptance requirements. This is WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented evidence, not a conformance claim.

## Commerce boundary

Price, stock and customer type are server-authoritative. Product cards use integer Persian-grouped amounts with the explicit unit `تومان`. Out-of-stock does not offer a false purchase action. Price changes replace stale data visibly. Wholesale pricing may appear only for an authoritative approved wholesale session and must carry a text label; retail and wholesale state are never inferred from visual styling. Quote, minimum quantity and purchase behavior remain owned by later gates and existing backend contracts.
