# Step 55-C — Product Evaluation Wireframes

**Status:** COMPLETE / GATE PASS candidate — Step 55 remains IN PROGRESS; 55-D is next.

## Scope and authority

55-C starts from canonical `main` baseline `8e9501c7d489cfee143851e4e1a043442ee64f09`. It owns exactly five frozen surfaces: Product Detail (`SF-C-01`), Product Media (`SF-C-02`), Compare Selection (`SF-C-03`), Compare Table (`SF-C-04`) and Wishlist/Alert Actions (`SF-C-05`). Step 53 journeys `SJ-01`, `SJ-02`, `SJ-03`, `SJ-11` and `SJ-12`, Step 54 component contracts and the Step 55-A/B shell remain authoritative.

The machine-readable source is `step55-product-evaluation-wireframes.json`. The deterministic generator writes `step55-wireframes/C/`; CI compares every generated byte. Repository evidence is canonical. Figma is an optional non-blocking mirror and cannot replace the source, frames, traceability or acceptance evidence.

## Product detail and media

The detail order is title/category, approved neutral media region, authoritative integer-Toman price and stock, Variant selection, key facts, primary continuation and comparison/customer actions. Out-of-stock disables only the affected continuation and explains why. Price change requires explicit acknowledgement before continuation. Wholesale price is never shown without an authenticated approved context.

Media is keyboard-operable and never gesture-only. Tabs, textual descriptions, previous/next controls and return to product facts remain available. Unavailable 3D/video content is not silently replaced or represented as successful; trusted text and available media remain usable. No product image, logo, video or 3D asset is invented in this low-fidelity gate.

## Comparison

Comparison accepts at most four compatible products from one category. The fifth selection is disabled with a reason and a bounded remove-first recovery. Category conflict fails closed while preserving valid prior choices. At 320/360px, equivalent product cards and labelled attribute lists replace a two-dimensional table; at wider widths, headers and cells retain explicit association and bounded horizontal behavior.

Price, stock and availability remain authoritative values, not copied snapshots owned by the comparison UI. Refresh announces changed facts. Removing a product updates count, focus destination and the resulting card/table state.

## Wishlist and alerts

Wishlist and product alerts are customer-owned resources. A guest is routed through OTP while the intended action is safely retained; successful authentication requires the action to be confirmed against current product state. Submitting, success and recovery are textually announced, duplicate submission is idempotent, and neither resource becomes a second source of price or stock truth.

## Responsive and accessibility evidence

Every screen has all three required states at 320px and one expanded primary state at 1440px. Traceability records behavior at 320, 360, 600, 840, 1200 and 1440px plus 400% zoom. Persian RTL reading/focus order, long-copy wrapping, bidi-isolated identifiers, 44×44 CSS-pixel targets, non-color status, one H1, landmarks and bounded recovery are explicit. Visual QA corrected right-edge RTL clipping, mobile title wrapping and overlapping media controls before acceptance.

## Boundary

55-C changes product-design contracts, generated low-fidelity evidence, validators, tests and state documentation only. It introduces no frontend/backend runtime, router, endpoint, migration, dependency, permission, business rule, production provider, Admin screen, high-fidelity UI, usability claim or prototype. Cart/checkout/payment remain owned by 55-D.
